## Source articles

PLOS Biology XML in `data2/xml/` retains the original articles' CC BY 4.0 licences, copyright notices, and attribution. Included JSON in `data2/train/` and intermediate JSON regenerated in `data2/processed/` contain extracted text from those articles and follow the same licences. XML-to-JSON conversion normalizes whitespace, extracts metadata and body paragraphs, and omits some publisher markup. Full original author lists, copyright holders, licence notices, and DOI links for all 57 articles are provided in [PLOS_ATTRIBUTION.md](PLOS_ATTRIBUTION.md). Keep that notice with the extracted article text and PLOS summaries, or provide equivalent attribution. The XML references figures and supplementary files; their images and attachments are not distributed here.

AI4Mat full texts are not distributed. Titles and source links in [metadata/ai4mat.json](metadata/ai4mat.json) identify the original papers; inclusion of this metadata does not relicense those papers. Quoted third-party material retains its original rights. In particular, the CC BY 4.0 grant covers only rights held by the study authors; it does not grant permissions for passages copied from AI4Mat source papers.

The MIT licence at the repository root applies to the code; it does not replace the content and article licences described above.
